4. INSURANCE

The Borrower shall maintain Takaful (Islamic) insurance over the Wakala Asset and charged land with DIB Bank Kenya Limited named as first loss payee. The insurer must be from the Bank's approved Takaful panel. Conventional insurance is not acceptable for this facility. Original Takaful policy schedules must be delivered to the Bank's Legal Department before drawdown and upon each annual renewal.

The Borrower shall maintain comprehensive insurance over all charged assets with DIB Bank Kenya Limited named as first loss payee (not merely noted). The insurer must be from the Bank's general approved panel. Original policy schedules must be delivered to Legal before drawdown and upon each annual renewal. Each policy must contain a thirty (30) day prior cancellation notice clause.
